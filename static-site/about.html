My Story

Beyond the Skills

How I Got Here

I've always liked working with hardware, taking things apart and figuring out how they work. Cybersecurity was the direction that let me keep doing that while building toward an actual career. The idea of hacking, understanding how systems could be broken and defended, was what pulled me in. A teacher early on helped me build the basic IT and cyber skills that got things started, and it grew from there.

How I Approach the Work

When I'm investigating something, I'm running through the same questions every time: who, what, where, when, why, and how. It's less like solving one problem and more like a chain of puzzles, where finding one answer is what lets you go find the next one. That's the part I actually enjoy, each piece you uncover opens up the next piece you need.

Where This Is Going

I'm not trying to specialize into one narrow lane. My goal is to become a jack of all trades across cybersecurity, comfortable moving between SOC work, investigation, and hands-on hardware, rather than getting boxed into just one discipline.
